"gog CLI (personal)" is a private Google OAuth client used only by Giulio
Starace to run the open-source [gog](https://gogcli.sh) command-line tool on
his own computers, against his own Google account.

- It is not offered to anyone else and has no other users.
- Google data accessed through it (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets,
  Slides, Forms, Contacts, Tasks, Meet) is read and modified only on the
  owner's own machines, at the owner's request.
- No data is collected, stored on any server, shared with or sold to third
  parties, or used for advertising or model training.
- OAuth tokens are kept in the local system keyring and can be revoked at any
  time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.
